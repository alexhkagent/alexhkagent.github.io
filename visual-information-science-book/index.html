What this book is这是一本什么书

Every screen is a controlled experiment on the human visual system, run millions of times a day without a consent form. A headline competes with a badge; a chart competes with a cookie banner; a face competes with a countdown timer. What you see is the result of that competition, not a neutral recording of the page. 每一块屏幕，都是一场针对人类视觉系统的受控实验，每天在未经同意的情况下运行数百万次。标题与角标竞争，图表与 Cookie 弹窗竞争，人脸与倒计时竞争。你看见了什么，是这场竞争的结果，而不是对页面的中立记录。

This book follows the signal from the photon to the click: the eye, the cortex, attention, color, motion, layout, and finally the devices and bodies that receive it all. It is written for designers, researchers, and anyone who has ever wondered why a page that looks fine can still fail the person reading it. 本书沿着信号从光子走到点击：眼睛、皮层、注意、颜色、运动、版式，以及最终承载这一切的设备与身体。它写给设计师、研究者，也写给任何好奇“为什么一个看起来没问题的页面，仍然会让读者失败”的人。

The argument is simple: perception is inference, attention is scarce, and design is the practice of choosing what the inference will be about. 核心论点很简单：知觉是一种推断，注意是一种稀缺资源，而设计，就是选择让这种推断指向何处的实践。

How to read如何阅读

01

In order按顺序

The chapters build: environment, attention, biology, color, motion, information, adaptation. Each one assumes the last.各章层层递进：环境、注意、生理、颜色、运动、信息、适应。每一章都建立在前一章之上。

02

By figure循图而读

Every chapter has at least one figure that can be read on its own. Start there if you are in a hurry.每章至少有一幅可独立阅读的图。时间有限时，不妨从图开始。

03

At the screen对着屏幕读

Two demos ask you to look, not just read. The book is about seeing; it should be tested with your eyes.书中有两个演示需要你亲自去看，而不只是读。这是一本关于“看”的书，应该用眼睛来检验。

About the figures. All diagrams are original vector illustrations drawn for this book. Classical illusion figures are redrawn from the public domain. Photographs are not used; every image is a diagram, because the subject is the mechanism, not the brand. 关于插图。所有图表均为本书原创的矢量插图。经典视错觉图依据公有领域资料重绘。书中不使用照片；每一幅图都是示意图，因为本书关心的是机制，而非品牌。

About the claims. Citations point to peer-reviewed work where it exists. Where the evidence is mixed, the text says so. Effect sizes in labs do not automatically transfer to the web; treat every number as a starting point for testing. 关于论断。凡有同行评审研究之处，均给出引用。证据不一致时，正文会明确说明。实验室中的效应量不会自动迁移到网页；请把每一个数字当作测试的起点，而非结论。
